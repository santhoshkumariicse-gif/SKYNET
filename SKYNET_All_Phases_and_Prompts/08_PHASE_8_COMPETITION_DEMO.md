# Phase 8 — First-Prize Competition Demo
## Demo Story
1. Show SKYNET Command Center with several devices.
2. Show normal baseline behavior.
3. Introduce a controlled, harmless test event.
4. Show anomaly detection.
5. Show AI explanation and evidence.
6. Show correlated investigation.
7. Show prediction/trend insight.
8. Show a proposed response with approval gate.
9. End with architecture and measurable results.

## Demo Narrator Prompt
Create a 3–5 minute technical demo script for SKYNET. Make every claim traceable
to a feature actually demonstrated. Highlight the problem, live evidence, AI
reasoning, privacy/local-first design, measurable impact and roadmap. Avoid hype
and avoid claiming unsupported accuracy.
